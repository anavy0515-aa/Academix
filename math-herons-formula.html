📐 Heron's Formula

Heron's formula is used to find the area of a triangle when all three sides are known.

s = (a + b + c) / 2
Area = √[s(s − a)(s − b)(s − c)]

Here a, b and c are the three sides and s is the semi-perimeter.

🧠 Important Points

• First find the semi-perimeter.

• Then substitute the values in Heron's formula.

• Keep units consistent.

• Area is expressed in square units.

✍️ Solved Examples

Example: Find the area of a triangle with sides 3 cm, 4 cm and 5 cm.

s = (3 + 4 + 5) / 2
s = 12 / 2 = 6 cm

Area = √[6(6−3)(6−4)(6−5)]
= √(6 × 3 × 2 × 1)
= √36
= 6 cm²

⚡ Quick Revision

Semi-perimeter = Half of perimeter
Area = √[s(s−a)(s−b)(s−c)]
