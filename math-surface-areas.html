📚 Basic Idea

Surface area measures the total area covered by the surfaces of a solid.

Volume measures the space occupied by a solid.

📦 Cuboid

LSA = 2h(l + b)
TSA = 2(lb + bh + hl)
Volume = lbh

l = length, b = breadth, h = height.

🧊 Cube

LSA = 4a²
TSA = 6a²
Volume = a³

a = side of cube.

🥫 Cylinder

CSA = 2πrh
TSA = 2πr(r + h)
Volume = πr²h

✍️ Solved Examples

Example 1:

Cube side = 5 cm.
Volume = a³
= 5³
= 125 cm³
Example 2:

Cuboid has l = 5 cm, b = 4 cm and h = 3 cm.

Volume = lbh
= 5 × 4 × 3
= 60 cm³
Example 3:

Cube side = 4 cm.
TSA = 6a²
= 6 × 16
= 96 cm²
