Coordinate Geometry

Complete Notes • Cartesian Plane • Coordinates • Quadrants • Quiz

📖 1. Introduction

Coordinate Geometry is the study of geometry using numbers and algebra. It helps us locate points on a plane using coordinates.

The system used to locate a point is called the Cartesian Coordinate System.

📐 2. Cartesian Plane

A Cartesian plane consists of two perpendicular number lines:

  • X-axis — horizontal axis
  • Y-axis — vertical axis
  • Origin — point where the two axes intersect
Origin = (0, 0)
Remember:

X-axis → horizontal
Y-axis → vertical
Origin → (0, 0)

🔢 3. Coordinates of a Point

The position of a point is represented by an ordered pair (x, y).

Point = (x, y)

The first coordinate is called the x-coordinate or abscissa.

The second coordinate is called the y-coordinate or ordinate.

Example:

For point P(4, 3):

x-coordinate = 4
y-coordinate = 3

📍 4. Abscissa and Ordinate

Abscissa

The x-coordinate of a point is called its abscissa.

Ordinate

The y-coordinate of a point is called its ordinate.

For P(-5, 7):

Abscissa = -5
Ordinate = 7

🧭 5. Quadrants

The x-axis and y-axis divide the Cartesian plane into four regions called quadrants.

First Quadrant → (+, +)
Second Quadrant → (-, +)
Third Quadrant → (-, -)
Fourth Quadrant → (+, -)
Easy way to remember:

I → + +
II → - +
III → - -
IV → + -

📌 6. Points on the Axes

Point on X-axis

For every point on the x-axis, the y-coordinate is zero.

(x, 0)
Examples:

(5, 0)
(-3, 0)
(10, 0)

Point on Y-axis

For every point on the y-axis, the x-coordinate is zero.

(0, y)
Examples:

(0, 5)
(0, -3)
(0, 10)

⭐ 7. Origin

The point where the x-axis and y-axis intersect is called the origin.

Origin = (0, 0)

The origin does not lie in any quadrant.

🧮 8. Important Examples

Example 1:

In which quadrant does P(3, 5) lie?

Both coordinates are positive.
Therefore, P(3,5) lies in the First Quadrant.
Example 2:

In which quadrant does Q(-4, 6) lie?

x is negative and y is positive.
Therefore, Q(-4,6) lies in the Second Quadrant.
Example 3:

In which quadrant does R(-2,-7) lie?

Both coordinates are negative.
Therefore, R(-2,-7) lies in the Third Quadrant.
Example 4:

In which quadrant does S(5,-3) lie?

x is positive and y is negative.
Therefore, S(5,-3) lies in the Fourth Quadrant.

⭐ 9. Important Points for Exam

  • Coordinates are written in the order (x, y).
  • The x-coordinate is called the abscissa.
  • The y-coordinate is called the ordinate.
  • The origin is (0,0).
  • Every point on the x-axis has y-coordinate 0.
  • Every point on the y-axis has x-coordinate 0.
  • Points in the first quadrant have (+,+) coordinates.
  • Points in the second quadrant have (-,+) coordinates.
  • Points in the third quadrant have (-,-) coordinates.
  • Points in the fourth quadrant have (+,-) coordinates.
  • The origin does not belong to any quadrant.
