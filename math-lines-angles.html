📚 Basic Concepts

A line extends endlessly in both directions, while a line segment has two fixed endpoints. A ray has one endpoint and extends indefinitely in one direction.

An angle is formed when two rays have a common endpoint. The common endpoint is called the vertex.

Complementary angles → Sum = 90°
Supplementary angles → Sum = 180°
Angles around a point → Sum = 360°

🧠 Important Properties

1. Linear Pair: Two adjacent angles whose non-common arms form a straight line have sum 180°.

2. Vertically Opposite Angles: When two lines intersect, vertically opposite angles are equal.

3. Corresponding Angles: When a transversal cuts two parallel lines, corresponding angles are equal.

4. Alternate Interior Angles: For parallel lines, alternate interior angles are equal.

5. Co-interior Angles: Interior angles on the same side of a transversal have sum 180°.

📐 Types of Angles

• Acute angle: greater than 0° and less than 90°.

• Right angle: exactly 90°.

• Obtuse angle: greater than 90° and less than 180°.

• Straight angle: exactly 180°.

• Reflex angle: greater than 180° and less than 360°.

✍️ Solved Examples

Example 1:

One angle of a linear pair is 65°.

Other angle = 180° − 65°
= 115°
Example 2:

Two vertically opposite angles are formed. If one angle is 72°, the opposite angle is also:

72°
Example 3:

Two complementary angles are 37° and x.

x + 37° = 90°
x = 90° − 37°
x = 53°
Example 4:

Angles around a point are 80°, 100°, 70° and x.

80 + 100 + 70 + x = 360
250 + x = 360
x = 110°
