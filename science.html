CHAPTER-WISE NOTES

Important Science Topics

Quick revision notes written in simple language.

01

Matter in Our Surroundings

Matter, particles, states of matter, temperature and change of state.

Key Points
  • Matter is made of tiny particles.
  • Particles have spaces between them.
  • Temperature affects particle motion.
  • Matter can change its state.
02

Is Matter Around Us Pure?

Mixtures, solutions, suspensions, colloids and separation methods.

Key Points
  • Mixtures contain two or more substances.
  • Solutions are homogeneous mixtures.
  • Suspensions have larger particles.
  • Filtration can separate insoluble solids.
03

Atoms and Molecules

Atoms, molecules, symbols, formulae and chemical calculations.

Key Points
  • Atoms are basic units of elements.
  • Molecules contain bonded atoms.
  • Chemical formulae represent substances.
  • Relative atomic mass is useful in calculations.
04

Structure of the Atom

Electrons, protons, neutrons, atomic number and mass number.

Key Points
  • Protons have positive charge.
  • Electrons have negative charge.
  • Neutrons have no charge.
  • Atomic number equals number of protons.
