📚 Basic Concepts

A circle is the set of all points in a plane that are at the same distance from a fixed point.

The fixed point is called the centre and the fixed distance is called the radius.

Diameter = 2 × Radius
Radius = Diameter ÷ 2

A chord is a line segment joining any two points on a circle.

A diameter is the longest chord of a circle.

🧠 Important Properties

• Equal chords of a circle are equidistant from the centre.

• Chords equidistant from the centre are equal.

• The perpendicular from the centre to a chord bisects the chord.

• Equal chords subtend equal angles at the centre.

• Equal angles at the centre correspond to equal chords.

🔵 Important Terms

Radius: Centre to any point on circle.

Diameter: Chord passing through centre.

Chord: Segment joining two points on circle.

Arc: Part of circumference.

Sector: Region enclosed by two radii and an arc.

Segment: Region enclosed by a chord and an arc.

✍️ Solved Examples

Example 1:

Radius = 7 cm.
Diameter = 2 × 7
= 14 cm
Example 2:

Diameter = 20 cm.
Radius = 20 ÷ 2
= 10 cm
Example 3:

A perpendicular from the centre to a chord bisects the chord.
Therefore, if the chord is 12 cm, each half is:
12 ÷ 2 = 6 cm
