📊 What is Statistics?

Statistics deals with the collection, organisation, presentation and interpretation of data.

Data means a collection of information or observations.

Data can be represented using tables, bar graphs, histograms and other graphical methods.

🧠 Mean, Median and Mode

Mean = Sum of observations ÷ Number of observations

Median: The middle observation when data is arranged in ascending or descending order.

Mode: The observation occurring most frequently.

📋 Example Data

Observation 2 4 6 8 10

Sum = 2 + 4 + 6 + 8 + 10 = 30

Number of observations = 5

Mean = 30 ÷ 5 = 6

✍️ Solved Examples

Example 1:

Find mean of 5, 10, 15.

Mean = (5 + 10 + 15) ÷ 3
= 30 ÷ 3
= 10
Example 2:

Find median of 3, 7, 5, 9, 1.

Arrange: 1, 3, 5, 7, 9
Middle value = 5
Example 3:

Data: 2, 3, 3, 4, 5.
The most frequent value is 3.
Therefore mode = 3.
