📚 Important Science Notes

Important definitions, concepts, formulas and exam-focused points for quick revision.

🧪 Matter

Matter: Anything that has mass and occupies space is called matter.

States of Matter

  • Solid: Fixed shape and fixed volume.
  • Liquid: Fixed volume but no fixed shape.
  • Gas: Neither fixed shape nor fixed volume.

Important Concept

The particles of matter are continuously moving and have spaces between them.

🧫 Cell

Cell: The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life.

Important Organelles

  • Nucleus: Controls cell activities.
  • Mitochondria: Produces energy.
  • Ribosomes: Help in protein synthesis.
  • Vacuoles: Store various substances.
  • Cell membrane: Controls movement of substances.

🌱 Tissues

Tissue: A group of similar cells performing a specific function is called a tissue.

Plant Tissues

  • Meristematic tissue
  • Permanent tissue

Animal Tissues

  • Epithelial tissue
  • Connective tissue
  • Muscular tissue
  • Nervous tissue

🚗 Motion

Motion: An object is said to be in motion when its position changes with respect to a reference point.

Important Formulas

Speed = Distance / Time
Velocity = Displacement / Time
Acceleration = Change in velocity / Time

⚡ Force and Laws of Motion

Force: A push or pull acting on an object is called force.

Newton's Laws

  • First Law: An object remains at rest or in uniform motion unless acted upon by an external force.
  • Second Law: Force is related to the rate of change of momentum.
  • Third Law: Every action has an equal and opposite reaction.
F = ma

🌍 Gravitation

Gravitation: The force of attraction between any two objects having mass is called gravitation.
F = Gm₁m₂ / r²

Important Point

The acceleration due to gravity near Earth's surface is approximately 9.8 m/s².

⚛️ Structure of Atom

Nucleus
  • Proton: Positive charge.
  • Electron: Negative charge.
  • Neutron: No electric charge.
