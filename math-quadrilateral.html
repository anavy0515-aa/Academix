📚 What is a Quadrilateral?

A quadrilateral is a closed figure made up of four line segments.

Sum of interior angles of a quadrilateral = 360°

It has four sides, four vertices and four interior angles.

🧠 Types and Properties

Parallelogram: Opposite sides are parallel and equal.

Rectangle: Opposite sides are equal and all angles are 90°.

Square: All four sides are equal and all angles are 90°.

Rhombus: All four sides are equal.

Trapezium: One pair of opposite sides is parallel.

Kite: Two pairs of adjacent sides are equal.

In a parallelogram, opposite angles are equal.
Adjacent angles of a parallelogram are supplementary.
Diagonals of a parallelogram bisect each other.

🔷 Important Facts

• Every square is a rectangle.

• Every square is a rhombus.

• A rectangle is not necessarily a square.

• A rhombus is not necessarily a square.

• The diagonals of a rectangle are equal.

• The diagonals of a rhombus are perpendicular bisectors of each other.

✍️ Solved Examples

Example 1:

Three angles of a quadrilateral are 80°, 90° and 100°.

Fourth angle = 360° − (80° + 90° + 100°)
= 360° − 270°
= 90°
Example 2:

One angle of a parallelogram is 70°.

Opposite angle = 70°
Adjacent angle = 180° − 70° = 110°
